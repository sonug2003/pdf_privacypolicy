Section 05

AI Assistant — Powered by Apple Intelligence, On Your Device

The AI Assistant runs on Apple Intelligence, using Apple's Foundation Models framework — the language model built into your device's operating system. It is a device capability, not a service we operate.

  • The model lives on your device. Your device downloads it once from Apple as a system component — we neither ship it nor download it — and it then runs locally on your device's Neural Engine.
  • It works with no internet connection. Once the model is on your device you can switch on Airplane Mode and the assistant still answers. If it can't work offline, it simply doesn't work — there's no online fallback.
  • Nothing you type or attach is sent anywhere. Your prompts, any documents you attach, and the assistant's replies are processed entirely on your device — not to us, to Apple, to Private Cloud Compute, or to any other AI service. The App makes no network request for AI, and contains no code that could.
  • It is off until you turn it on. The assistant is disabled by default, and you can switch it off any time in Settings → AI Assistant.

What It Can See

Only what it needs to answer the message in front of it: your chat messages in the current conversation, and text from any document you explicitly attach with the paperclip (having a file on your device does not expose it). All of this stays in memory for the conversation and is cleared when you start a new chat — none of it is logged, sent anywhere, or used to train any model.

Personalization (Opt-In)

If you turn personalization on, the App may notice a detail you type in chat (name, email, phone, organisation) and ask before saving it, so drafts can use your real details. Saved details stay in the App's private on-device storage only — you can view, edit, or erase them anytime in Settings → AI Assistant → Personalization.

ℹ️

Apple Intelligence requires a supported device and system version, and must be enabled in Settings. If it's unavailable on your device, the AI Assistant is simply unavailable — every other tool in the App works normally.

Section 14

Data Security

We take reasonable technical precautions to protect the data stored by the App:

  • All data is stored using Apple's established system APIs (UserDefaults, FileManager, Security), benefiting from the full iOS/macOS security architecture — App Sandboxing, hardware Data Protection, and Secure Enclave.
  • Passwords for password-protected PDFs you open, and passwords/PINs for certificates you sign with, are held in memory only for that one action and are never written to disk, Keychain, or settings.
  • Cryptographic keys generated on-device use RSA-2048 via Apple's Security framework and, where ephemeral, are never persisted.
  • SHA-256 hashing (via CommonCrypto) is used for document integrity verification.
  • The App transmits no document content or personal file data over any network connection. Ad requests and anonymous analytics events (Section 19) are the only network traffic the App makes, and neither ever includes anything from your documents.
⚠️

No method of data storage is 100% secure. While we protect your device-resident data, we cannot guarantee absolute security against physical device theft. Use your device's Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode to protect your device.

Section 19

Advertising, Analytics & Tracking

Advertising

Pyrbit PDF shows ads from Google AdMob only to free (non-subscriber) users, and only after they've already used a Pro tool's one free daily use — watching a short ad unlocks one more use of that tool that day. Subscribers never see an ad anywhere in the App, and tools that aren't Pro-only never show ads.

To show and measure ads, AdMob collects a device advertising identifier. On iOS you're asked whether Pyrbit PDF can use this identifier for personalized advertising (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) — you can decline, and the App and its ads work exactly the same either way. We do not receive or store this identifier ourselves; it's collected and used by Google under Google's own Privacy Policy.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand overall app usage — which tools get used, how often, and on which device type (iPhone, iPad, or Mac) — so we know what to build next and what to fix.

Firebase Analytics collects a randomly-generated installation identifier (not your name, Apple ID, or email), your device platform and OS version, and the names of a small set of in-app events (e.g. "a document was opened," "a document was exported") — never the contents of your documents, their filenames, or anything you type. This data is processed under Google's own Privacy Policy and is not linked by us to your Apple ID or sign-in name.

Cookies & Browser-Based Tracking

Pyrbit PDF is a native iOS/iPadOS/macOS application. It does not use a web browser engine, does not load web pages, and does not deploy cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, fingerprinting scripts, or any other browser-based tracking technology. The advertising identifier described above is the only cross-app signal involved, and it exists solely for the ad SDK named above.

📄

Everything else the App does — document processing, OCR, editing, signing, redaction, and everything the on-device AI Assistant does — never involves any ad network, analytics SDK, or third-party service, and none of it leaves your device.

Section 20

Do Not Track

"Do Not Track" is a browser-level signal, and Pyrbit PDF is a native application rather than a website, so there's no browser DNT signal for it to respond to. The equivalent, meaningful control on iOS is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (Section 12), which the App fully honors: declining it stops AdMob from personalizing ads to you, immediately and permanently, without affecting anything else in the App.
