Introduction

At Pyrbit, we believe your documents are your business — not ours. PDF Studio was built with a single guiding principle: everything happens on your device, and your files never leave it.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, how it is stored, and what choices you have. Please read it carefully. By downloading or using PDF Studio, you agree to the practices described here.

Section 04

Information You Provide

a) Display Name

The App allows you to set a personal display name in your profile. It is stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults and never transmitted anywhere. It is entirely optional — the default is "User" and you can change it anytime.

b) Aadhaar Identity Information

The Aadhaar Digital Signature tool lets you embed a cryptographic signature into a PDF using your identity details. When you use this feature, you provide:

👤
Full Name
As it appears on your Aadhaar card
Local only
🪪

Displayed masked — only last 4 digits visible in UI
Local only
📅

Used to build the X.509 certificate identity field
Local only
⚧
Gender
Included in certificate subject field
Local only
⚠️

Important: Your identity data is permanently embedded in the X.509 certificate inside the signed PDF you create. If you share that PDF with others, the certificate — including your name and masked Aadhaar — will be readable by anyone who inspects the signature. You are responsible for how you share signed documents.

c) Support Communications

If you tap Send Feedback, your email client opens a pre-filled email to pyrbit.dev@gmail.com. Any information you include is received solely to respond to your inquiry and is never used for marketing.

Section 13

Data Security

We take reasonable technical precautions to protect the data stored by the App:

  • All data is stored using Apple's established system APIs (UserDefaults, FileManager, Security), which benefit from the full iOS/macOS security architecture including App Sandboxing, hardware Data Protection (device encryption), and Secure Enclave capabilities.
  • Cryptographic keys generated for the Aadhaar Digital Signature feature use RSA-2048 via Apple's Security framework and are held in memory only during the signing session. They are not persisted to the Keychain or any file afterward.
  • SHA-256 hashing (via CommonCrypto) is used for document integrity verification.
  • The App transmits no personal data over any network connection — eliminating the risk of interception in transit.
⚠️

No method of data storage is 100% secure. While we protect your device-resident data, we cannot guarantee absolute security against physical device theft. Use your device's Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode to protect your device.

Section 14

International Data Transfers

Because PDF Studio stores all data locally on your device and does not operate a backend server, we do not transfer personal data internationally in the ordinary course of business.

The only instances in which data may cross borders:

  • Apple StoreKit transactions — Apple routes App Store purchases through its global infrastructure, governed by Apple's privacy practices.
  • Support email — Emails to pyrbit.dev@gmail.com travel through Google's (Gmail) email infrastructure, which may span multiple countries. Google has adopted EU Standard Contractual Clauses as an appropriate transfer safeguard.

Children's Privacy

PDF Studio is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable under local law, such as in the EU under GDPR).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Aadhaar Digital Signature feature involves government-issued identity numbers and should only be used by adults, or by minors under parental supervision.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this App (e.g., via the support email), please contact us at pyrbit.dev@gmail.com and we will promptly delete that information.
